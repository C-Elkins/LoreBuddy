# LoreBuddy Project Philosophy

These principles guide product decisions, implementation choices, and lore
contributions across LoreBuddy.

## 1. LoreBuddy Is Contextual

LoreBuddy should provide the right piece of context at the right moment. It
should help the player understand what they are seeing without dumping an
encyclopedia on them.

## 2. LoreBuddy Is Optional

The player controls how much lore they see. LoreBuddy should support quiet play,
light hints, and deeper exploration without forcing a single level of detail.

## 3. LoreBuddy Does Not Spoil Unnecessarily

LoreBuddy should not reveal future story information unless the player asks for
it. Explanations should respect the player's current progress and discovery
context.

## 4. LoreBuddy Remembers

LoreBuddy tracks what the player has already discovered. This memory should
allow explanations to build naturally over time without repeating information
or assuming knowledge the player has not encountered.

## 5. LoreBuddy Distinguishes Fact from Interpretation

LoreBuddy clearly separates confirmed lore from inference, interpretation,
theory, and community speculation. Uncertainty should be visible rather than
presented as established fact.

## 6. LoreBuddy Is Source-Aware

Every lore entry should have provenance. LoreBuddy should preserve enough source
information for players and contributors to understand where an entry came from,
how reliable it is, and what attribution or licensing applies.

## 7. LoreBuddy Works Offline

The core addon should not require an internet connection. Online services may
provide optional enhancements, but the essential contextual lore experience
must remain available offline.

## 8. AI Is Optional

AI can help explain lore, find connections, or adapt an explanation to the
player. AI is not the source of truth. Lore data, provenance, and clearly marked
uncertainty remain authoritative over generated responses.
